Patched with **Morphe CLI** & **$SOURCE Patches**.  
Supports both **Non-Root** devices and **Root** managers (Magisk / KernelSU / APatch).

### ✨ Features
- 🎧 **Background Playback**: Listen with screen turned off or in the background without YouTube Premium.
- 🚫 **No Ads**: Audio and video advertisements are completely blocked.
- ⚡ **Always High Audio Bitrate**: Forces maximum audio stream quality by default.
- 🎤 **Synchronized Lyrics**: Real-time synced lyrics from 15+ third-party lyric databases.
- 🔀 **Track Crossfade**: True dual-player seamless crossfading between consecutive songs.
- 🚗 **Android Auto**: Certificate spoofing enabled for in-car dashboard playback.
- 🛡️ **SponsorBlock**: Automatically skip non-music intros, outros, and sponsor segments in music videos.
- 🖤 **AMOLED Pure Black Theme**: Deep true black background (`#000000`) for OLED power savings.
- 👎 **Return YouTube Dislike**: Displays dislike counts on music videos and tracks.
